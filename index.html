About Me

A systems engineer passionate about turning complex data into reliable, actionable systems that businesses can trust.

The Journey

I started my career curious about how companies like Netflix and Uber process millions of events in real-time. That curiosity led me to data engineering—where I found my passion for building systems that never sleep.

Today, as a Systems Engineer at Tata Consultancy Services, I work with enterprise scale data systems. My proudest achievement? Re-architecting a critical Power BI reporting system using Azure Databricks, which reduced report load times by 70% and eliminated system crashes.

I also built a self-monitoring data pipeline framework that catches errors before they become problems, maintaining 99.9% data integrity across production systems.

Before TCS, I explored the intersection of AI and data at Atomstate Technologies (building NLP sentiment engines) and Zummit Infolabs (extracting insights from unstructured text).
